# Third-Party Data Notices

## English word-frequency list

The 3,000-word ranked list in `src/data/commonEnglishWords.json` is derived from
`wordfreq` 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer. The word-frequency data is licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

- Source: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Citation: Robyn Speer (2022), `rspeer/wordfreq` v3.0.2,
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7199437

The list is ordered by estimated frequency across multiple corpora. Frequency
coverage varies by corpus and context; it does not guarantee that these 3,000
words account for a fixed percentage of all English.
